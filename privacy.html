Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version

iShufl, provided by Swift Dev Co, does not collect, share or sell personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to a server run by us.

Your music and lyrics

The audio files and lyrics you import are copied into the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. They are removed if you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Your name, skins, stickers and settings

The name you enter during setup, your custom skins, stickers, wallpapers and preferences are saved on your device only.

Photos

If you add a photo as a sticker or wallpaper, you choose it in the system photo picker, and iShufl receives only the photo you select. Cutting out a sticker's subject happens on your device.

Motion sensors

Some foil stickers shimmer as you tilt your phone. The motion data is read on your device to move the shine and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

iShufl Unlimited is a monthly subscription (with a free trial for eligible Apple Accounts) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; iShufl never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Network and live radio

iShufl does not connect to any server of its own and contains no third-party code that does. Only if you use Radio, the app asks the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info) for a list of stations and then plays the station's own stream. Like any website or app that loads something from the internet, the directory and the radio station can see your IP address. iShufl sends them no account, name or other personal information. Stations are provided by third parties, and some may carry mature content; you can hide a station from the player with the Report button.

Reports by email

If you choose to email a report about a radio station, the email is sent from your own mail app and contains the station's name, country and ID that you can see before sending. We use it only to look into that station.

Children

Because iShufl collects nothing, it collects no information from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact swiftdevco@proton.me.
