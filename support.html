Support

Need help with iShufl?

Email swiftdevco@proton.me and include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing. I aim to reply within a few days.

Adding your music

iShufl plays audio files you already have. Save them to the Files app or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop them from a Mac, then open the menu, choose Songs and tap Add. MP3, M4A, WAV and other common formats work.

iShufl Unlimited

Unlimited removes the free limits on songs, skins and stickers and unlocks every radio genre and favorites. Choose a monthly subscription (with a free trial if you are eligible) or a one-time lifetime purchase. To restore a purchase, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. To cancel a subscription or request a refund, use your Apple Account settings or reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy requests

iShufl keeps your data on your device, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For any privacy question, use the same address with the subject "Privacy request".
