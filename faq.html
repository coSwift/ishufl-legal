Frequently asked questions

How do I get support?

Email swiftdevco@proton.me. I aim to reply within a few days.

Where is my music stored?

Only on your device, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app removes it.

Does iShufl have an account I can delete?

No. There is no account and nothing is stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete.

Does iShufl collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does Radio need the internet?

Live stations are streamed from the stations' own servers. Your own music plays offline.

A radio station has content I don't want. What can I do?

While the station is playing, tap the flag button next to Favorite. You can hide the station for good and, if you like, email a report.

Can I change the app icon or redo the setup?

Yes: Settings has App Icon, and Settings → About → Show Welcome Tour.
